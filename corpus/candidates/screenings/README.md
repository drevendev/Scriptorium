# Corpus qualification screenings

This directory records source-free decisions made **before** Scriptorium spends work freezing or analyzing a candidate for the calibration, benchmark, or author-profile corpus.

A screening may reject a work even when legal/public source evidence is strong. The current corpus rule is absolute: calibration, parity-benchmark, and author-profile corpus entries must contain at least **300,000 characters including spaces**. A FantLab result below that floor cannot satisfy M2 merely by finding a source match later.

Screenings are not corpus admissions and do not contain literary source text. They preserve the evidence that changed the project decision, plus any edition/provenance caveats needed to prevent a future short-input showcase from being mistaken for parity evidence.
